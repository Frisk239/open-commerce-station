# Theme and content, not a page builder

The Merchant goes live by selecting a Theme, editing content, uploading Products and images, and turning on connections such as payment. They do not design arbitrary page layouts. “空白模板” means empty Catalog and copy, not missing features and not a website builder.

**Status:** accepted

**Considered Options:** free-form page builder; fixed pages with content only; Theme plus content and Catalog

**Consequences:** `chanpin/` should prototype Theme pick, content editing, Catalog upload, and go-live settings — not a drag-and-drop canvas. A Theme marketplace is out of scope until we deliberately add it.
