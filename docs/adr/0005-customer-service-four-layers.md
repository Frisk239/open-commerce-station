# Customer service is four layers; ship contact and Inbox first

A complete Independent Station includes Storefront Contact, Inbox, Order-aware Support, and self-serve FAQ/order status. The product is designed as all four. The first build and `chanpin/` prototypes may ship Storefront Contact plus Inbox first; Order-aware Support (order, tracking, refund in the thread) stays on the roadmap, not as an accidental plugin.

Staff work CS inside the Merchant Portal. The Merchant does not deploy a separate Chatwoot-class host to go live. Chatwoot is a reference for shape.

**Status:** accepted

**Considered Options:** paste-a-script only; Inbox-only email; full Gorgias-class on day one; four-layer product with Contact+Inbox first

**Consequences:** Prototypes must show a Storefront entry and a Portal Inbox. Do not prototype CS as a third app login. Refund-from-thread can wait, but the Inbox layout should leave room for order context.
